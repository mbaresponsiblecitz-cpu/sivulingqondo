# Sivulingqondo Website — Editing Guide

This website updates itself when you edit content. **You never touch code.**
You log in, change words, numbers or photos, click **Publish**, and the live site
updates on its own in about 1–2 minutes.

---

## 1. Logging in

1. Go to **https://sivulingqondo.netlify.app/admin/**
   (or **https://sivulingqondo.org.za/admin/** once the domain is connected)
2. Click **Login with GitHub** and approve.

> You need a GitHub account that has been invited to the project. If login says
> you don't have access, ask the site administrator to invite you.

---

## 2. How editing works (the golden rules)

- Find the section in the left-hand menu, click it, change the field, click **Publish**.
- Changes go live in **1–2 minutes**. If you don't see them, wait and refresh.
- You can leave most fields blank and the site keeps its original wording — nothing breaks.
- **Photos:** use clear, landscape (wide) photos where possible. Keep captions short.
- When in doubt, change one thing, publish, and check the live site before doing more.

---

## 3. The menu (what each section controls)

### Site & Campaign Settings
The shared things that appear across the whole site:
- **Organisation name, tagline, phone, WhatsApp number, email, address**
- **WhatsApp pre-filled message** — the text that appears when someone taps the green WhatsApp button
- **Facebook / ForGood links**
- **NPO / PBO numbers**
- **Footer note** — the small line at the very bottom of every page
- **Partners & funders** — the names in the logo strip (add/remove freely)
- **Programme ticker** — the scrolling strip of programme names on the home page
- **Campaign** — this is where you update the **Amount raised so far** (the donation
  bar), the goal, the budget line items, and the impact tiers (e.g. R350 = a chair).
- **Impact stats** — the three big numbers (children in ECD, programmes, years)

### Page Content
The words and photos for each page. Each page is its own screen:
- **Home Page** — hero headline & intro, the 4 hero photos, campaign card text,
  section headings, the story & quote, transparency text, and the get-involved band.
- **About Page** — Story, Mission, Team and Transparency text.
- **Campaign Page** — the appeal paragraph, headings, and the "before any purchases" text.
- **Get Involved Page** — the four cards (add/remove/edit title, text, button).
- **Contact Page** — heading and the form button label.

### Programmes (Focus Areas)
Each programme card. Add a new one with **New Focus Areas / Programmes**:
title, summary, photo, and an emoji icon.

### Team Members
Add/edit people: name, role, photo.

### Gallery Photos
Add photos with a caption and (optionally) the programme they belong to.

### Transparency Wall
"What we bought" proof: title, photo, an optional tag (e.g. "Weekly"),
an invoice link, and a date.

## 4. The most common tasks

**Update the donation total (the progress bar)**
Site & Campaign Settings → **Campaign** → **Amount raised so far (Rand)** →
type the new number → **Publish**.

**Add a gallery photo**
Gallery Photos → **New Gallery Photos** → upload the image, add a caption → **Publish**.

**Post proof of a purchase (transparency wall)**
Transparency Wall → **New** → title (e.g. "15 chairs delivered"), upload a photo,
paste an invoice link if you have one → **Publish**.

**Change the home page headline or a hero photo**
Page Content → **Home Page** → Hero → edit the headline, or replace a Collage photo →
**Publish**.

**Add or edit a programme**
Programmes → pick one, or **New** → fill in title, summary, photo, emoji → **Publish**.

**Add a team member**
Team Members → **New** → name, role, photo → **Publish**.

---

## 5. Formatting text (bold, links)

Some longer text boxes (Story, Mission, the Campaign appeal) support simple
formatting:
- **Bold:** wrap text in `**two stars**` → **like this**
- **Link:** `[text to show](https://the-link.co.za)`
- New paragraph: leave a blank line.

Plain typing is completely fine — formatting is optional.

---

## 6. If something looks wrong

- Wait 2 minutes and refresh — it may still be publishing.
- If a field looks broken, clear it and re-type, then Publish. Empty fields fall back
  to the original wording, so you can't permanently break a page.
- For anything you can't fix from the editor, contact the site administrator.
